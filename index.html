Projects That Make a Difference

Seven innovative concepts designed to solve real-world problems using technology and artificial intelligence.

PROJECT 01 🎓

Smart School

A smart platform that transforms school life into a connected digital experience.

  • Attendance using fingerprint or QR code.
  • Student grades and performance tracking.
  • Class schedules and exam timetables.
  • Helpful notifications for students.
  • An AI assistant that answers curriculum questions.
PROJECT 02 📚

AI Study Buddy

An AI-powered study companion that makes learning easier and more organized.

  • Take a photo of a textbook page.
  • Turn the page into a clear summary.
  • Generate multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
  • Explain difficult topics step by step.
  • Create a personalized study schedule.
PROJECT 05 🤟

Sign Language AI

A communication assistant designed to help deaf and hard-of-hearing people connect.

  • Recognize selected hand signs using a camera.
  • Convert recognized signs into text.
  • Present text as visual sign-language content.
  • Support accessible everyday communication.
PROJECT 06 🚀

Future Career

A guidance platform that helps students discover careers matching their interests and strengths.

  • Skills and interests assessment.
  • Suggestions for suitable study fields.
  • Explore emerging and future careers.
  • Recommended courses to develop skills.
PROJECT 09 🥽

AI Translator Glasses

A smart-glasses concept that helps people understand languages and the world around them.

  • Display live speech translations.
  • Make conversations across languages easier.
  • Show useful information about nearby objects.
  • Prototype using a camera and display.
PROJECT 11 🏛️

Digital Museum

An interactive online museum bringing history and cultural heritage closer to everyone.

  • Explore exhibits through virtual tours.
  • Discover stories about historical artifacts.
  • Use QR codes to learn about each item.
  • Ask an AI guide about the history of exhibits.
PROJECT 12 🔎

Find My Lost Item

A lost-and-found platform that makes it easier to reunite people with their belongings.

  • Upload a photo of a lost item.
  • Record where and when an item was found.
  • Search for items using images.
  • Match lost reports with found-item reports.
