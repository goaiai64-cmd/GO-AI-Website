Four intelligent concepts designed to make learning, planning, and discovery smarter.

PROJECT 01 🎓

Smart School

المدرسة الذكية

A connected school platform that transforms everyday school management into a simple, smart digital experience for students, teachers, and administrators.

KEY FEATURES
  • Student grades, academic progress, and performance tracking.
  • Class schedules, lesson timetables, and exam calendars.
  • Important announcements and notifications for students.
  • An AI assistant that answers questions about school subjects and curriculum content.

PROJECT 02 📚

AI Study Buddy

مساعد المذاكرة الذكي

An AI-powered study companion that helps students understand lessons, revise more effectively, and organize their study time.

KEY FEATURES
  • Take or upload a photo of a page from a textbook.
  • Turn textbook pages into clear, easy-to-understand summaries.
  • Automatically generate multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
  • Explain difficult concepts step by step in simpler language.
  • Create a personalized study timetable and revision plan.

PROJECT 11 🏛️

Digital Museum

المتحف الرقمي

An interactive digital museum that brings history and cultural heritage closer to everyone through virtual exploration and intelligent explanations.

KEY FEATURES
  • Virtual tours that let visitors explore museum spaces online.
  • Informative stories and details about historical artifacts.

  • An AI museum guide that explains the history and significance of exhibits.
